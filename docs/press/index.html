FM-STEP

A synth groovebox that runs on the Akai MPC Sample.

Eight tracks of synthesis, a 64-step sequencer and live performance effects, launched from the SD card next to the stock firmware. Nothing is flashed; the normal MPC is always one exit away.

01 · The idea

A sampler and a groovebox in one box

Most mods for the MPC Sample extend it as a sampler. FM-STEP turns the same hardware into a synth groovebox: sound engines you play and sequence step by step, with an interface built for jamming live. The plan is a hybrid instrument, with sample tracks joining the synth tracks.

The MPC Sample as it ships

  • Sampler engine with real-time sequencer, swing and automation
  • No step sequencing
  • No synth engine
  • 16 pads, 3 knobs, encoder, 2.4" screen

Source: Synth Anatomy, Oct 2026

With FM-STEP running

  • 8 synth tracks on 23 sound models, each with its own sound chain
  • 16 patterns of up to 64 steps, with locks, swing, timing nudges and chains
  • 8 fader performance effects and pad toggles for live jamming
  • Live graphics for every parameter on the 2.4" screen
  • Back to stock with SHIFT + STOP, STOP
02 · Features

What's in v0.9

Everything below runs on the MPC Sample hardware today, on firmware 1.3.0.12.

Sound

  • 23 models from Mutable Instruments' Plaits (MIT): analog, FM, DX7 banks, chords, modal, drums
  • Sub oscillator, drive ladder filter, tempo-synced wobble
  • 3-band EQ per track, from +18 dB to a DJ-style kill
  • Dub tape delay with throws, space echo, reverb
  • Warmth and tape: drift, chorus, glue, saturation, hiss, crackle

Sequencer

  • 8 tracks, each drum on its own track
  • 16 patterns × up to 64 steps, chains of up to 8
  • Pitch, velocity, length and ±45% timing per step
  • Up to 4 parameter locks per step; live recording of notes and knob moves
  • Swing, quantise, mutes, live length pads that stay on the beat

Performance

  • Fader effects: low pass, high pass, slicer, stutter, space echo, reverb wash, pan, volume
  • Pick any effect from any screen; switch each on or off from the pads
  • One-press reset of any knob to neutral
  • Two-step STOP: let the echoes ring, or silence everything

Interface

  • Its own drawing engine: anti-aliased curves, arc knobs, dot-matrix numbers
  • Live EQ, filter, envelope, delay, reverb and tape graphs
  • Dot-grid steps: size and brightness show velocity, capsules show long notes
  • Pads and views follow the playhead; instructions are built into the screen

Safety

  • Launched from the SD card; nothing flashed or overwritten
  • Firmware check and file integrity check before launch
  • Watchdog, crash restart, and a breaker that falls back to stock
  • Projects autosaved to the card every 30 s

Extras

  • Audio over USB-C to a computer or phone (works; level still being tuned)
  • 16 project slots and 4 sound banks on the card
  • Offline renderer for auditioning the engine on a computer

06 · How it's made

Made by an artist, for playing out

FM-STEP is built by Darren Alexander Cole, a moving-image and sound artist studying computational media in the Interactive Media & Game Development (IMGD) department at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI). Cole has more than 20 years of experience as an artist working between sound and moving image, from 16mm film projects to augmented and virtual reality experiences.

Made with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI, this custom mod for the MPC Sample grows out of a lifelong love of exploring the roots of hip hop: jazz groove sessions and deep-listening modular sets that leave you wondering whether you just left Jamaica or are on a flight to Europe. Every change has been tested on the MPC, and a slow beta-testing phase will follow. FM-STEP is where live performance meets the kinetic energy of the sample-based producer's loop.

Engine

  • C++ sound engine and sequencer clock in a real-time audio thread, 5 ms blocks

Interface

  • Go app with its own drawing engine, Inter typeface and dot-matrix text

Safety

  • Supervisor with watchdog and breaker; stock always one exit away
