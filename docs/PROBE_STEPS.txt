FM-STEP PROBE - first run on the MPC Sample (about 3 minutes)

1. Back up the SD card on your Mac.
2. Unzip fmstep-...-probe-sd.zip. Copy the "autorun" and "FMSTEP" folders
   to the top level of the SD card.
3. In Terminal run:  dot_clean "/Volumes/<your card name>"
   (removes the hidden ._ files macOS adds), then eject the card.
4. MPC off. Insert card. Power on. Answer "Start FM-STEP" at the prompt.
5. The screen should say FM-STEP PROBE and the pads should turn blue.
   For 90 seconds: press every pad softly and hard, press every button,
   turn K1 K2 K3, volume and fader, turn and push the DATA encoder.
6. The MPC returns to the normal software by itself.
7. Power off, put the card in the Mac, send back the whole FMSTEP/logs folder.

If anything looks wrong: hold POWER until off, remove the card, power on.
If no prompt appears at step 4: send FMSTEP/logs anyway (it may be empty -
that tells us the runner did not start our script).
